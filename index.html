02 / 12

The split

You have the team. We have the lists.

Great data will still convert average callers. Great callers cannot save a dead list. Both of us put something real on the table.

You

Three callers and two closers to start. People who can actually work a seller. That is not a small thing to put up.

Us

The software, and the six kinds of distressed-seller records we pull from county and city filings. Pre-foreclosure, probate, eviction, pre-lien, code violation, fire damage.

03 / 12

Not a skip-trace dump

This is not Goliath. It is not Data Syndicate.

We already built exclusive county seats. Two investors per county. Hundreds to a thousand a month. Locked a quarter to a year. If every seat filled, that product is six figures a month in subscriptions. We are not guessing that the lists have value.

Exclusive seats

Not a free-for-all. When a county is taken, it is taken. That is the machine we already sell.

County seats, not everyone

$497 to $997 a month depending on the county. City code data is separate. We do not spray the same file to a thousand dialers.

Honest

We are not at full speed on every county and every code-violation city yet. Automation is still catching up. When all six types are running, the ceiling is your team — not the lists. Full-speed data can feed 30 callers. We start at 3.

04 / 12

Cream of the crop

What we actually hand a caller.

Not every filing. The ones a person can still talk to, with enough meat on the bone — or a path even when the bone is thin.

40%+ equity

In the home. We are not sending you underwater files and calling it a list.

A person or a trust

100% independent or trust-owned. No banks. No servicers. Someone who can say yes.

Distressed, or still worth the call

A beat-up house, or a well-kept house we can still call. Distress is not the only door.

05 / 12

Pre-foreclosure

Fat equity or thin. We can work both.

Fat equity is a cash or assignment conversation. Thin equity is where subject-to earns its keep. Cash, subto, seller-finance — and we can get a reno done if that is the path.

Fat equity

Room for an assignment. The caller is not begging a seller to take a lowball so we can survive the fee.

Thin equity

Subject-to, seller-finance, or a reno if the house needs it. We do not throw those files away just because cash will not fit.
