The handshake

Thirty days. No long-term decision on day one.

We suggest 3 callers and 2 closers unless you want to put up more. We thought we would start there. After 30 days we pick 3, 6, or 12 months — and we decide if we expand the team, or we decide we do not work together.

Days 1–30

Same team. Same lists. We see what actually closes. Not a spreadsheet guess.

Then we choose

3, 6, or 12. We also decide whether to grow the team. We want to reinvest for max contracts per month.

Or we don’t

We shake hands and stop. We doubt that — given your team and our data — but 30 days exists so nobody is guessing.

First 30 days

You get paid first. That month only, on deal one.

You put up the team. On the first wholesale assignment we bring in during those 30 days, your team fee comes off the top, then you still take 10% of that assignment fee.

Off the top

$5,070

Three callers + two closers. Paid from deal one, first.

Then 10% of that fee

$6,570

On a $15,000 assignment: $5,070 + $1,500. That is the total in your pocket on deal one.

Every other deal we bring in that first month still pays you 10% of the assignment fee. This is good faith. We know this works. Paying you first is not an issue — there are plenty of deals. It is a 30-day run to see if the partnership makes sense. If we both show up, we want to run it and build a max team.

Why you · why we stay

We could hire our own team. It would stall everything that actually makes us money.

The data is good enough that we could stand up a floor in six months and get by. We do not specialize in being a VA, or in training VAs. We specialize in acquisitions, underwriting, closing, getting deals sold, and data. Your team takes over a piece of that. The hard part is training people who understand this industry, and we do not want to build that.

We want you

We are talking to a few teams. We want this one. We do not want to wait another week or another month.

Why we stay partners

Every month we spend trying to specialize in something we could hand to someone like you pushes both teams back. We scale faster if each of us stays on what we are great at.

Software and sales keep moving

If we divert into hiring and training, the lists slow down and the deals slow down. That helps nobody in this room.
